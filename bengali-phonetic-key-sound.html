click a word to hear it, then practise typing it
Caret-safe: click or arrow anywhere. Typing a letter appends to the word at the caret; Backspace edits the roman buffer (pops the last typed letter), even mid-word.
Conjuncts fuse automatically: ny → ন্য · cch → চ্ছ · kS → ক্ষ · prIti → প্রীতি
' releases the inherent vowel (k'thA → কথা, j'ny → জন্য); ্ inserts a hasanta; Space commits a word.
